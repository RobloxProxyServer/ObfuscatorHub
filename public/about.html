// What is ObfuscatorHub?
Script Protection Made Simple

ObfuscatorHub lets you protect your Lua scripts by converting them into a secure, password-locked loadstring. Share the loadstring — not your code. Anyone can run your script in Roblox or Delta, but nobody can read or steal the source.

Password Protection
Every script is locked with a password you choose. Only someone with that password can open the viewer and see, edit, or delete the original code. Everyone else just gets the loadstring.
Instant Loadstring
Get a loadstring in seconds. Paste it in your executor, share it publicly, or post it in Discord. Anyone can run it in Roblox — but nobody can read the source code inside.
Edit Your Script Anytime
Need to update your script? Open the viewer page, enter your password, and edit the code directly in your browser. Save and the loadstring URL stays exactly the same — no resharing needed.
Download the Source
Download your original script as a .lua file straight from the viewer. Just enter your password and hit Download. Perfect for keeping local backups of your work.
Delete Anytime
Want to take a script offline? Delete it permanently from the viewer with your password. The loadstring stops working instantly and the code is gone for good.
Discord Bot
Protect scripts from Discord using /obfuscator. Supports 15+ languages automatically so your whole community can use it worldwide without ever opening the website.
Dashboard
Sign in with Discord, Roblox, Google, GitHub, or Microsoft to see all your protected scripts in one place. View, edit, delete, and download any of them from your personal dashboard.
Strong Protection
Your script is stored securely behind your password. No one else can access, copy, or steal the source. Only you control it.
Always Free
No rate limits, no premium tiers, no credit card ever. Protecting, editing, downloading, and deleting your scripts is and always will be completely free.
